# White Rock, Black Cinder

A three-day truck-and-ATV trip proposal — one night on the sandstone at
White Pocket in the Vermilion Cliffs, one night on the volcanic cinder of
the Cinder Hills outside Flagstaff, and home on the third day.

A single self-contained `index.html`: every photo is embedded, so it works
offline once loaded. Arrow keys or swipe to move between slides.

Photo credits appear on each slide (Bureau of Land Management and public
domain sources; individual CC-licensed photos attributed inline).
